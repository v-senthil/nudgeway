# Send to a group

Sending to a group works exactly like sending to a 1:1 contact — use the composer at the bottom of the group thread in your Inbox.

## How to use

1. Open **Inbox**.
2. In the conversation list, click the group you want to message. Group threads have a group icon and the group's subject line.
3. Type your message in the composer at the bottom.
4. Click **Send** (or press Enter).

The message will appear in the thread with a "sending" pill, then flip to "sent", "delivered", and finally "read" as WhatsApp reports back.

## What you can send

The same message types as a 1:1 chat:

- Plain text.
- A template — click the template button next to the composer, pick a template, and fill in variables. See [Send a template message](#/inbox/send-template).
- Images, videos, audio, documents, and stickers — click the attachment icon.

Messages send in the order you press Send. Two back-to-back texts arrive at WhatsApp in that order.

## The 24-hour window

The same WhatsApp rule that applies to 1:1 chats applies to groups: you can only send free-form text if someone in the group has messaged you within the last 24 hours. Outside that window you must use an approved template. If you try to send free-form text after the window has closed, the message flips to "failed" with a red banner.

## Troubleshooting

- **Message stuck on "sending" and then flips to "failed" with a red banner** — hover the failure indicator to see the reason. The most common causes are the 24-hour window having closed (send a template instead) or a malformed template payload (re-pick the template).
- **Composer shows "read only" and disables the Send button** — this can happen if your business number does not hold admin rights on the group and the group is configured admin-only. The group creator needs to grant admin, or invite you to a group that allows all members to post.
- **Message failed with "business account restriction"** — your WABA is throttled or under Meta review. Contact your Meta representative; no user action fixes this on the Nudgeway side.
- **Group not in the conversation list** — run **Sync** on the [Groups page](#/groups/list-sync) to pull the latest roster.
